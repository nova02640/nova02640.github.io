## Forsaking the Far, Returning to Dust; At Home in the Smoke



From this day on, be a person returned to dust

Clear the social feeds, bid farewell to a hundred distant shows

From this day on, cast off every longing for far-off places

And only grip the everyday at hand—grounded, plain, and white



From now on, cut off all the world's surging tides

No chasing trends, no gossip, no moss of stars

Let influencers rise and fall, let opinions churn and flip

All the clamor of the faraway—I push it all aside



Tired of watching other people's brilliance through a screen

Seeing through the faraway's false gaiety and swell

No longer moved by sorrows and joys of strangers

Just longing to be bound to dawn and dusk, keeping watch over smoke and ash



I thought that if I shed every outward hue

And turned to the people and affairs beside me—three meals, coming and going

I could catch the truest warmth of the human world

And smooth the wandering weariness long lodged in my heart



## Weary of Stillness, Gazing Afar; Back to the Sea of People



But ordinary smoke and fire slowly grind down expectation

Dawn returns to dusk, and days grow only plain and pale

The people around me stay unchanged, day after day

Leaving only heaps of idle time for staring blankly, for pacing

I am curious about the vastness of mountains and rivers, the hundred faces of the world

Greedy for the freshness and surprise the distance brings

Knowing full well that though the faraway gleams, I can never arrive

While the years beside me stay the same—always hard to let go



Half the heart clings to the smoke, content in the dust at arm's length

Half the heart sails toward mountains and seas, coveting the world's splendor

At last I drift, numb in the near, dreaming of the far

Keeping watch over ordinary years, gazing at the surging sea of people
